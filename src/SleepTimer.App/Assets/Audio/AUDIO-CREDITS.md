# Sleep Timer audio credits

The three music loops and three end cues in this folder were procedurally synthesized for the Sleep Timer project by `tools/generate_sleep_audio.py` in October 2026. They contain no third-party recordings, samples, or music loops. The MP3 files are encodings of those original generated waveforms; the WAV cues are the generated waveforms directly.

These assets are intended to be distributed with Sleep Timer. No separate third-party audio license or attribution is required.
